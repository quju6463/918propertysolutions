Cash purchase option

Sell as-is
without open homes.

If you want a simpler sale, we may consider buying suitable Wellington properties directly for cash. This can suit owners who want privacy, certainty, flexible timing or a way around repairs they do not want to take on.

We are comfortable reviewing properties with deferred maintenance, structural concerns or unfinished work. Any direct purchase is still subject to standard property review, due diligence and commercial feasibility, with no obligation on either side.

How it works

A simple way
to test your options.

You do not need a full report before contacting us. Start with the property and what you are trying to decide.

1

Send the address

Share the address or suburb, your contact details and anything useful: photos, listing link, current use, condition or timing.

2

We review the options

We look at as-is sale potential, renovation risk, resale value, rental outcome, holding costs and whether a direct purchase may fit.

3

You choose the path

That might be selling, renovating, renting, holding, getting specialist advice or discussing timing if we are interested in buying.

About 918 Property Solutions

Local property operators,
not just a form.

918 Property Solutions brings together trade experience from a licensed building practitioner and financial discipline from a chartered accountant.

We are Wellington-based property operators who also do our own projects, so we think practically about cost, resale value, rental potential, timing and risk.

Our building experience helps us assess repair risk realistically from the start. Our accounting background helps us test the numbers carefully, move decisively where a property fits, and be clear about timing, assumptions and conditions.

If we are interested in buying or being commercially involved in a property, we will say so clearly. You should still get your own legal, tax, valuation, finance or real estate advice where needed.
